# PART A — Curriculum & Competency Research
*Source: `@market_researcher`, grounded in AICPA/NASBA and top-university catalogs.*

## A1. CPA exam structure (CPA Evolution, Jan 2024+)

A joint AICPA + NASBA model. Candidates pass **3 Core sections** + **1 Discipline of their choice**. Every section combines multiple-choice questions and task-based simulations; data/technology skills are tested across all sections.

**Core (mandatory):**
- **AUD — Auditing & Attestation:** ethics, professional responsibility, risk assessment, evidence, forming conclusions.
- **FAR — Financial Accounting & Reporting:** GAAP reporting framework for business and state/local government entities.
- **REG — Taxation & Regulation:** federal taxation of individuals and entities, business law, professional ethics.

**Discipline (choose one):**
- **BAR — Business Analysis & Reporting:** advanced technical accounting, financial-statement analysis, technical research (builds on FAR; ~42% pass rate).
- **ISC — Information Systems & Controls:** IT audit, data management, security, SOC reporting (builds on AUD; ~68%).
- **TCP — Tax Compliance & Planning:** advanced tax compliance & planning, complex entity tax (builds on REG; ~78%).

*Sources: [AICPA CPA Evolution](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-insights/article/navigating-cpa-evolutions-new-model-for-the-cpa-exam); [Uniform CPA Exam Blueprints](https://dlcp.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/DLCP/publication/attachments/Uniform%20CPA%20Examination%20Blueprints%202.5.24.pdf); [Becker discipline guide](https://www.becker.com/blog/cpa/how-to-choose-your-cpa-exam-discipline-section).*

## A2. University curriculum backbone

Reviewing top-tier programs (UT Austin, BYU, Illinois, USC) plus public catalogs (NC State, CSUN, Albany, MTU) reveals a consistent topic backbone moving from principles → professional judgment:

1. Introductory Financial Accounting (the accounting cycle; the "language of business")
2. Introductory Managerial Accounting (cost behavior; internal decisions)
3. Intermediate Financial Accounting I & II/III (deep FAR: assets, liabilities, equity, revenue recognition)
4. Cost / Advanced Managerial (budgeting, variance analysis, strategic cost)
5. Accounting Information Systems (ERP, internal controls — accounting × technology)
6. Federal Taxation (individual & corporate)
7. Auditing & Assurance (standards, evidence, reporting)
8. Ethics & Professional Responsibilities (integrated or capstone) + internship/capstone

*Sources: [UT Austin BBA Accounting](https://catalog.utexas.edu/undergraduate/business/degrees-and-programs/bachelor-of-business-administration/accounting/); [BYU Marriott](https://marriott.byu.edu/acc/bsacc/what-will-i-study/program-overview/); [NC State Accounting BS](https://catalog.ncsu.edu/undergraduate/management/accounting/accounting-bs/accounting-bs.pdf); [CSUN catalog](https://catalog.csun.edu/academics/ACCT/courses).*

## A3. Modular competency map (à la carte)

Each module is independently enrollable, has its own mastery meter and badge, and maps to CPA section(s) for those who want the full path.

| Module | Learning objectives | Key skills | Practice tasks (in BOG) | CPA map |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Accounts Receivable** | Manage credit-to-cash | Aging, allowance for doubtful accts | Record sales, process payments, reconcile sub-ledger | FAR / BAR |
| **Accounts Payable** | Manage procure-to-pay | 3-way match, accruals | Enter bills, schedule payments, accrue at close | FAR |
| **General Ledger** | Maintain the source of truth | JE prep, trial balance, COA | Post & correct JEs, adjusting entries | FAR |
| **Bank Reconciliation** | Cash accuracy & control | Timing differences, fraud detection | Match statement to GL, record fees/interest | AUD / FAR |
| **Inventory & COGS** | Value goods & cost of sales | FIFO/LIFO/avg, LCM/NRV | Value inventory, compute COGS, margin analysis | FAR / BAR |
| **Payroll** | Workforce compensation | Withholding, accruals, compliance | Gross-to-net, file forms, record liabilities | REG / FAR |
| **Fixed Assets** | Track capital investments | Depreciation, capitalize vs. expense | Set up assets, depreciate, dispose/impair | FAR |
| **Financial Statements** | Synthesize data into reports | Prepare BS/IS/CF/equity, ratios | Assemble statements, footnotes, analysis | FAR / BAR |
| **Period Close** | Finalize the books | Cutoff, accruals/deferrals | Run month-end checklist, verify sub-ledgers | FAR / AUD |
| **Audit Basics** | Verify data integrity | Evidence, control testing | Vouch/trace transactions, risk assessment | AUD / ISC |
| **Tax Basics** | Regulatory obligations | Nexus, book-vs-tax income | Simple returns, estimated payments | REG / TCP |
| **Managerial / Cost** | Drive internal performance | Variance, CVP, budgeting | Product costing, overhead, forecasting | BAR / FAR |

## A4. "Practice room" pedagogy (evidence-based)

- **Worked examples** (cognitive-load theory): show step-by-step solutions before independent attempts.
- **Simulation / task-based learning:** reconcile a real statement or close a month — not just MCQs. (This is precisely the BOG practice-room model.)
- **Immediate feedback loops:** validate entries in real time so incorrect procedures aren't encoded.
- **Spaced practice:** revisit core concepts at increasing intervals for retention.
- **Mastery thresholds:** require high accuracy (e.g., 90%+) on a final simulation before a module is "proficient."

*Sources: [World Bank — Competency-Based Accounting Education](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/846871568609139631/pdf/Competency-based-Accounting-Education-Training-and-Certification-An-Implementation-Guide.pdf); [C-BEN — Moving Accounting Curricula Toward CBE](https://www.c-ben.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Moving-Accounting-Curricula-Toward-a-Competency-Based-Education-Model-July-2023.pdf).*

## A5. Mastery model for partial paths ("Competency Stack")

Three levels per module, so even a single-module learner gets a full arc:
1. **Technician (task-based)** — *the How:* can perform the task in a controlled simulation.
2. **Analyst (context-based)** — *the Why:* can explain the financial-statement impact.
3. **Professional (judgment-based)** — *the What-if:* handles exceptions, identifies risk, improves the process.

Credentials:
- **Micro-certificates** per module ("Accounts Payable Specialist").
- **Competency clusters** for related sets (AR + AP + Bank Rec + Payroll = "Financial Operations Specialist").
- **CPA readiness score** (opt-in) — dynamic % coverage of a CPA section as modules complete (e.g., "45% of FAR Core covered"). Guides, never gates.

# PART B — Separate-Identity Brand & Product Design
*Source: `@aesthetics_agent`, benchmarked against Brilliant, Khan Academy (Wonder Blocks), and Becker.*

## B1. Name — recommendation: **Pi Academy** ⭐

Inherits the `π` motif from BOG-Pi, introduces a new word ("Academy") that signals a new place, wordmarks cleanly, and deliberately avoids "CPA" in the name (many learners pursue one competency, not the full credential).
- **Tagline:** "Learn the discipline behind the ledger."
- **UI label:** always "Pi Academy" — never "BOG Academy," never just "Academy."
- Alternatives considered: Ledger Academy, BOG Institute, The Precision Lab, Decimal.

## B2. Personality & voice — *a senior CPA who wants you to win*

Distinct from BOG-the-tool (calm, precise, neutral). The Academy is **encouraging, momentum-driven, mentor-like** — conversational but never casual, no hype.
- **Do:** ownership language ("your AP competency is practice-ready"); name & normalize errors ("Good try — here's why the debit goes left"); treat partial paths as complete ("AR: Mastered"), never "37% complete."
- **Don't:** emoji-celebration in body copy, gate language ("you must complete the full program"), shame/loss mechanics ("streak broken"), mid-flow time estimates.

## B3. Visual system — *sibling, not twin*

Same structural language as BOG (spacing scale, radius, Inter/JetBrains Mono), but a **light-forward, warm-amber** surface instead of BOG's dark sidebar + electric-blue neon. **BOG neon is reserved for the practice-room hand-off** so crossing into the tool feels like a threshold.

**Architectural cornerstone — `[data-surface="academy"]` token scope:** the same `<Button>`/`<Card>`/`<Badge>` components adopt Academy colors by overriding CSS variables at the scope level (Khan Academy Wonder Blocks pattern). **Zero component duplication.**

- **Amber** = the Academy accent ("learning light"); **Sage** = mastery/completion ("grown, earned").
- **Six per-competency path colors** (`--acad-path-ar`, `-ap`, `-gl`, `-fs`, `-tax`, `-cost`) so each module stands alone with its own identity. AR = familiar BOG blue (feels like home); AP = violet; etc. **Lock these as invariants early.**
- **Type:** no new font — Inter at 700–800 with tighter tracking + new `acad-display/h2/h3` sizes.
- **Motion:** more expressive than BOG but purposeful (≤350ms), all wrapped in `prefers-reduced-motion` / `data-reduced-motion`. The **"Enter Practice Room" transition** (amber → BOG-dark overlay → workspace) is the single most important moment — build it first.

*Token block, Tailwind config, and component CSS provided in full in the `@aesthetics_agent` spec (see B6 implementation table; raw spec preserved in agent transcript).*

## B4. Key screens / IA

1. **Academy Home** — warm canvas, "Your competencies" status chips, one clear "Continue" card, "start a new competency." A subtle "[BOG →]" portal link, never the primary CTA.
2. **Module Picker (à la carte)** — color-coded competency cards (units, ~hours, progress). **No locks, no prerequisite language.** "Choose one or all — each module stands on its own."
3. **Module Detail / Path View** — vertical node map (lesson → quiz → "Practice in BOG"); current node highlighted; the "Practice in BOG" entry is themed (amber + neon chevron) to signal a different surface.
4. **Practice Room Hand-Off** — transition overlay into the BOG workspace; a persistent **32px amber context bar + breadcrumb** ("Pi Academy / AR / Unit 2") stays visible the whole session; "Done with practice →" returns to the exact node.
5. **Progress & Mastery** — status rows + per-module badges. Labels: *Not Started → In Progress → Practice-Ready → Mastered* (sage). **No "% to full CPA" framing**; no radar chart in v1 (it can make partial progress feel like a gap).

## B5. Separation signals (clarity without confusion)

| Moment | Signal | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| BOG sidebar entry | Amber "Pi Academy" item (only amber element) + tooltip | Color + copy |
| Page transition | Canvas lightens to warm white | `[data-surface]` swap |
| Academy nav | No BOG dark sidebar; amber wordmark | Layout + identity |
| In practice room | Persistent amber context bar + breadcrumb | Always-visible UI |
| Return to BOG | "Done with practice →" reverse transition | Button + animation |
| Routes | `/academy/*` vs `/app/*` | Clean separation |

# PART C — Synthesis & decisions for Human

**Where the two streams meet:** the curriculum's *task-based, immediate-feedback, mastery-threshold* pedagogy (A4/A5) is realized exactly by the design's *practice-room hand-off + per-module mastery* (B4). The à-la-carte competency map (A3) maps 1:1 onto the six path-color channels and module picker (B3/B4). Partial-path credentialing (A5) and the "partial paths are complete" voice/visual rules (B2/B5) reinforce the owner's core differentiator.

**Recommended M1 (one module, end-to-end):** ship **Accounts Receivable** — primer → BOG practice tasks → mastery meter + "AR Specialist" badge, behind a feature flag. It proves the separate-identity shell + practice-room bridge with the smallest surface.

**Decisions — locked by owner (2026-06-28):**
1. ✅ **Name:** **Pi Academy** — π is the program's main logo (amber-crown sibling of the BOG-Pi cube, `PiAcademyMark`).
2. ✅ **Brand separation:** own amber identity + own logo.
3. ✅ **Freemium:** free tier = minimum to register + one genuine win (AR taster + one real practice rep + first mastery move) as a hook; **all depth/breadth = paid Pi Academy Membership** (every module, unlimited practice, badges/micro-certificates, CPA-readiness). Free must feel impressive enough to drive enrollment.

**Still open:**
4. **First paid module:** confirm **Accounts Receivable** for M1.
5. **Practice data:** seeded practice companies vs. a learner's real ledger (privacy).
6. **Membership price** + build timing vs. core BOG GA.

Once you decide 1–4, `@technical_lead` ratifies the data model + practice-room bridge (Phase 0), then `@senior_frontend_engineer` starts B6 steps 1–3.
